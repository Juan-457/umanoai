Who we are

We understand the business before proposing technology

UmanoAI builds on the experience of its founder, Juan José Galván, who has more than 20 years of experience at multinational companies in Argentina's agricultural sector.

Today, we support distributors, formulators and other agricultural-input channel companies in their digital transformation, combining business knowledge with artificial intelligence, automation and digital solution development.

We also work with companies in other industries and provide training so their teams can apply these tools in their daily work.
