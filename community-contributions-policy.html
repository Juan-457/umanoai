Scope and effective date

How the commitment works

This policy begins on October 1, 2026 and is not retroactive.

  1. Initial period. From October through December 2026, UmanoAI will allocate 5% of revenue actually collected, excluding taxes.
  2. Annual commitment from 2027. The annual percentage will be between 1% and 5% of revenue actually collected, excluding taxes.
  3. Advance definition. The exact percentage will be defined and published before each year begins and will remain unchanged for that period.
  4. Recipient. Contributions will go to Banco de Alimentos Buenos Aires.
  5. Transparency. UmanoAI will report the total contributed at the end of each annual period and retain the corresponding receipts.
Important: this is UmanoAI's own commitment. It does not imply a partnership, agreement, sponsorship or endorsement by Banco de Alimentos Buenos Aires. At the start of this policy, UmanoAI is not reporting previous donations, meal counts, beneficiary counts or allocations to specific programs.
